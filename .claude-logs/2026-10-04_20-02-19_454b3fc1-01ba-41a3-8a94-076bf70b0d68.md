Built, tested, and committed so far:

Video upload, playback, timeline with ruler/playhead, click/drag/keyboard scrubbing
Manual trim (draggable handles, non-destructive, keyboard support)

Confirmation step: AI commands show a plan ("I'll keep X–Y") before applying, Apply/Cancel
Dark UI styling matching an approved mockup (indigo/cyan theme)
Automatic transcript generation (Deepgram)
Silence detection (7-second threshold, tuned to avoid flagging natural speech pauses)
Filler word detection ("um," "uh," "erm," "er," comma-separated filler "like")
One-click silence+filler cleanup with time estimate and one-step Undo
Manual per-filler cut editor (click a flag → waveform view → drag handles by ear → Cut/Cancel)

Key architecture decisions, with reasoning:

Gemini chosen originally for native audio/video understanding; gemini-3.5-flash-lite chosen for command parsing specifically because it's cheaper and sufficient for short-command-to-JSON parsing, still free-tier
Deepgram added only after Gemini's timestamp estimates were measured (via real benchmarking against ground-truth audio) and found too imprecise (250–700ms off) to safely cut fillers
Filler cuts use gap-detection (find real silence on both sides of a filler in the actual audio) rather than cutting at estimated/reported timestamps directly, because even Deepgram's timing has rounding/error large enough to clip real words; if no safe gap exists, the filler is left flagged for manual review rather than guessed at
