"Ad hoc" means made for one specific purpose, when needed, instead of set up permanently in advance.

In Theo's message ("we should have adhoc, optional staging, ephemeral envs") he means: no permanent staging environment that everyone shares. Instead, an engineer spins up a temporary staging environment only when a particular change needs one, uses it, and tears it down. "Ephemeral" is the same idea from the other side: the environment is short-lived by design.

That's why he pairs it with "optional." Gautam's point was that small fixes ship to prod after local testing and don't need staging. Ad hoc staging covers the occasional big change without making everyone pay for a staging tier all the time.
