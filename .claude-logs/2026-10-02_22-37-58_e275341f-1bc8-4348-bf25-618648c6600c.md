**The model, as built (`history.ts`, notes section 3c):**
- **Snapshots, not actions.** Each step is `{ trim, cuts, clips, replacements }`, the edit as it stood just before a change. The history is `{ past, future }`: a new edit pushes onto `past` and clears `future`, and Undo and Redo move the present between the two.
- **Re-record takes:** the snapshots that contain a take keep it, so Undo moves it into the redo branch and Redo brings back the same recording. It's freed only when a new edit after an undo clears the branch, the cap drops it, or a new video is loaded.
