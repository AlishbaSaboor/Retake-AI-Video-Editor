# Background music for Make it

Make it can lay one of the tracks in this folder under an edit, turned down
while someone speaks. It lists the audio files here (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`,
`.opus`, `.wav`, `.webm`) and lets Gemini pick one by its file name, so name
each file for its mood, e.g. `upbeat-pop.mp3`, `calm-piano.mp3`,
`playful-ukulele.mp3`, `energetic-electronic.mp3`.

**Only free-licence music goes here.** Every track must be one we may use,
change and ship in exported videos without paying or asking: CC0 / public
domain, the Pixabay Content License, or CC BY (then the credit below must
be kept and given wherever the video is published). No tracks taken from
YouTube, streaming services or anywhere the licence isn't clear.

While this folder holds no audio files, Make it plans without music and
says so; nothing fails.
